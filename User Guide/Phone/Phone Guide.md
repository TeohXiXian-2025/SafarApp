# Safar phone user guide

Captured from the [live Safar demo](https://safar-app-cristal-teohs-projects.vercel.app/demo) in Chrome at a 390 × 844 phone viewport on 10 October 2026. The fresh browser session had Safar site data and browser cache cleared, then the page was refreshed. These are phone-layout captures, separate from the [desktop/tablet guide](../Desktop%20Guide.md).

The screenshots show the live demo in Chrome. The Food search completed, and the Ichiran vote was submitted so the 2–2 split and decision options are visible. Booking uploads and Auto-plan application were not submitted during capture.

## Get started

1. On the landing page, tap **See a demo trip**. ![Phone landing page](00-landing.png)
2. Tap **Start the demo**. ![Phone demo introduction](00-demo-start.png)
3. The guest trip opens. Tap the **Trip Quest** bar to see the current step. ![Phone trip overview](00-trip-overview.png)

## 1. Start with the trip

Open **Trip → People**, then create an invite link.

![Step 1 prompt](01-start-with-the-trip-instructions.png)
![Step 1 People screen](01-start-with-the-trip-screen.png)

## 2. Meet the group

In **People**, review the four sample members and tap **View group preferences**.

![Step 2 prompt](02-meet-the-group-instructions.png)
![Step 2 People screen](02-meet-the-group-screen.png)

## 3. Add a teammate

In **People → Invite links**, copy the link to share with a teammate on a real trip.

The pictured demo link was revoked after capture. Create a new link for your trip.

![Step 3 prompt](03-add-a-teammate-instructions.png)
![Step 3 invite links](03-add-a-teammate-screen.png)

## 4. Review preferences

Check the group rules and tap **Edit my preferences** to review your needs.

![Step 4 prompt](04-review-preferences-instructions.png)
![Step 4 preferences screen](04-review-preferences-screen.png)

## 5. Add fixed bookings

Use **Trip → Bookings → Add ticket or hotel**. Sample flight, train and hotel documents are provided in **Demo files**. Try uploading one, review the extracted details, then save.

![Step 5 prompt](05-add-fixed-bookings-instructions.png)
![Step 5 Bookings screen](05-add-fixed-bookings-screen.png)

## 6. Find food with Halal Radar

Open **Food** near Kyoto Station. Compare the food labels and their evidence before adding a suitable place to Ideas. Confirm halal requirements with the restaurant.

![Step 6 prompt](06-find-food-with-halal-radar-instructions.png)
![Step 6 Food search results](06-find-food-with-halal-radar-screen.png)

## 7. Suggest a place or link

In **Ideas**, tap **Add place or link**, paste the sample social link provided in Trip Quest, and review the suggested places.

![Step 7 prompt](07-suggest-a-place-or-link-instructions.png)
![Step 7 Ideas screen](07-suggest-a-place-or-link-screen.png)

## 8. Vote Agree or Disagree

In **Ideas → Vote now**, scroll through the idea details to the **Agree** and **Disagree** buttons. Add a reason if a place conflicts with your needs.

![Step 8 prompt](08-vote-agree-or-disagree-instructions.png)
![Step 8 voting screen](08-vote-agree-or-disagree-screen.png)

## 9. Trigger a food conflict

Open Ichiran in **Vote now** and tap **Agree**. Read the food-rule warning, choose **I'll only have drinks / eat elsewhere**, then confirm. The result is **2 Agree / 2 Disagree**.

![Step 9 prompt](09-trigger-a-food-conflict-instructions.png)
![Step 9 Ichiran voting screen](09-trigger-a-food-conflict-screen.png)

## 10. Solve the conflict

After the vote creates a disagreement, open **Ideas → Needs a decision** and review the halal alternatives and regroup plan. The team lead decides whether to accept or reject the conflicted idea for the itinerary.

![Step 10 prompt](10-solve-the-conflict-instructions.png)
![Step 10 decision options after the vote](10-solve-the-conflict-screen.png)

## 11. Auto-plan the trip

In **Plan**, tap **Auto-plan → Whole trip** to let Safar suggest a schedule. Review the preview before applying it, or edit the timeline manually.

![Step 11 prompt](11-auto-plan-the-trip-instructions.png)
![Step 11 Plan screen](11-auto-plan-the-trip-screen.png)

## 12. Check prayer time

In **Plan**, inspect a prayer time, nearby facility and parallel activity for a teammate who does not pray.

![Step 12 prompt](12-check-prayer-time-instructions.png)
![Step 12 prayer-aware Plan screen](12-check-prayer-time-screen.png)

## 13. Final review

Review **Plan, Ideas, Food and Trip → People**. Use the quest's **Show** buttons to revisit completed steps.

![Step 13 prompt](13-final-review-instructions.png)

The guest trip is temporary. Tap **Keep trip** and create an account if you want to retain it.
