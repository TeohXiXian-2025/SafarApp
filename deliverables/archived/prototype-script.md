## 🎯 Pro Presentation Tips & Delivery Checklist

1. **Pacing is Key:** Practice speaking at a steady, rhythmic pace. Don't rush through the numbers in Slide 2 ($230B, 1.8B) or the problem in Slide 3—let the weight of the market and friction sink in.
2. **Body Language & Gestures:** Whenever mentioning the two parallel tracks (Slide 5 and Slide 6), use your left hand for the Muslim path and right hand for the non-Muslim companion path, then bring your hands together to indicate the **synchronized regroup pin**.
3. **Emphasis Words:** Punch the words: *"Zero Native OS"*, *"72%"*, *"Nobody sacrifices"*, and *"Live working product, not a mockup"*.
4. **Immediate Video Cue (Slide 11):** At the 2:42 mark, smoothly transition as the demo video begins playing.
